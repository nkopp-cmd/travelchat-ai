Sake, Sweets, and a Soak is a two-day Kyoto trip for a first-time visitor who wants to explore local culture, enjoy a traditional sweet, and finish with a bathhouse soak.

The plan moves from Fushimi’s shrine and sake lanes to Nishijin’s weaving district and a bath in Kita. Each stop has a suggested time and duration, so you can follow the day at an easy pace.

## Day 1: Fushimi’s Shrine and Sake Lanes

![Painted illustration for day 1 in Kyoto: Fushimi’s shrine and sake lanes](/media/trip-story/c29ccf3b0204f55dd2592f1aa988d9de695df4edc63ff136d8086e1ed7758460-1080.webp)

Begin at Goko-no-miya Shrine at 10:00 am for 40 minutes. This local shrine in Fushimi is a quiet place to start the day. Take a slow walk through the grounds, then pause at the main gate to look at its painted carvings. The grounds are free to visit. Find it at 174 Goko-no-miya Monzen-cho, Fushimi-ku, Kyoto.

At 1:30 pm, spend 35 minutes at Terada-ya, a historic inn associated with Sakamoto Ryoma. Tour the rooms and take a moment to notice how compact the building is. Admission costs a fee. The inn is at 263 Minamihamachou, Fushimi-ku, Kyoto.

Continue to Fushimi Sake District at 2:30 pm for 75 minutes. Stroll along the canal past the brewery buildings, and stop for a small sake tasting if you feel like it. Walking is free; tasting costs extra. The district is in Fushimi-ku, Kyoto.

## Day 2: Nishijin Lanes and a Kita Bath

![Painted illustration for day 2 in Kyoto: Nishijin lanes and a bath in Kita](/media/trip-story/ce4a5a37aee3c0f6a411ec2528edae29e56bd0b78499d79646069ab7c7207de6-1080.webp)

Start this afternoon in Nishijin District at 1:30 pm, setting aside 60 minutes to wander the side streets of this traditional weaving district. Look for woven fabrics in shop windows as you go. It is free to walk around. The district is in Nishijin, Kamigyo-ku, Kyoto.

At 3:00 pm, take 45 minutes for Kyoto Traditional Sweets. Choose a traditional sweet at a shop in Nishijin, and ask which one they make for the season. The cost varies by order, and shops are in various locations across Kyoto.

Finish at Funaoka Onsen at 5:30 pm, with 60 minutes for a warm soak in this historic bathhouse. Look for the old tilework and carved wood inside; its interior is a lovely part of the visit. Bath admission costs a fee. The bathhouse is at 82-1 Murasakino Minamifunaokacho, Kita-ku, Kyoto.
