Foreign Visa and Mastercard work widely in Singapore shops and restaurants, while some stalls and taxis may still need cash. For buses and MRT, tap a foreign Visa or Mastercard, or use an EZ-Link card; as of 2026-10, foreign cards incur a daily transit fee, with an Amex exception described below.

## Which cards can I use in shops and restaurants?

Use a foreign Visa or Mastercard as your first choice in shops and restaurants. They work widely, so you can usually pay by card for everyday purchases. Check with the merchant before ordering if you want to use Amex or UnionPay, since acceptance depends on the individual business.

For taxis, ask the driver whether cards are accepted and whether a surcharge applies before you leave. Many taxis accept cards, but acceptance and surcharges can vary. Keep cash as a backup in case your payment method is not accepted.

At hawker stalls, ask how to pay before you order. Some accept only cash or supported QR wallets, and seeing a card terminal at another stall or nearby business does not mean the stall you choose takes cards.

## How much cash should I carry?

Keep cash for hawker stalls, small purchases and taxi backup. A suggested reserve is SGD30–50 per day for hawker meals and modest market purchases. Carry SGD2, SGD5 and SGD10 notes; small notes make it easier to pay without running into change problems.

You do not need to rely on cash for every purchase. Use a card where it is accepted, then use your cash reserve for stalls or small transactions that need it. For more practical payment details, see Localley’s [Singapore payment guide](/help/singapore/pay).

## How do I pay for buses and MRT?

Tap a foreign Visa or Mastercard on buses and MRT. Supported phone wallets can also be used. As of 2026-10, foreign cards incur SGD0.60 per day. May 2025 brought transit access for foreign Amex cards and supported wallets without that administration fee.

Use the same card or device throughout your trip. That keeps your transit payments together under one payment method. If you start tapping your physical card, for example, keep using that card rather than switching to its phone wallet.

You can also buy an EZ-Link card at station service points and reload it with cash. Ticket offices refund eligible remaining value. Check whether the remaining value is eligible before you count on a refund.

## Can I use a QR wallet at hawker stalls?

Supported Alipay+ home wallets can pay participating merchants, including some hawker stalls. As of 2026-10, coverage included 25 overseas apps. Use an existing eligible wallet and look for its logo on the SGQR code before paying.

A PayNow-only code does not guarantee that a visitor can pay with a tourist wallet. Check the displayed logos or ask the merchant before placing your order, and carry cash in case the wallet is not supported.

## Where can I withdraw cash, and what should I check?

Foreign cards are accepted at ATMs operated by DBS/POSB, UOB and OCBC. Operator fees vary from zero to about SGD8, and your card issuer may also charge a fee. As of 2026-10, check the fee shown on screen before you accept the withdrawal.

Choose SGD when the ATM asks which currency to use, and cover your PIN as you enter it. Use a bank-attached machine when possible. These steps help you see the local-currency amount and keep your PIN private.

## Should I tip?

Tipping is not expected in Singapore. Restaurants and hotels often include a service charge, so check the bill rather than treating a tip as a required part of payment.

## FAQ

### Can I pay everywhere with a foreign Visa or Mastercard?

They work widely in shops and restaurants, but some hawker stalls accept only cash or supported QR wallets. Ask at the stall before ordering.

### Do I need cash for taxis?

Many taxis accept cards, sometimes with surcharges. Confirm card acceptance and any surcharge with the driver before departure, and keep cash as a backup.

### Does tapping a foreign card on transit cost extra?

Foreign Visa and Mastercard incur SGD0.60 per day. Foreign Amex cards and supported wallets gained transit access without that administration fee in May 2025; use the same card or device throughout.

### Can I use an ATM without paying a fee?

Operator fees vary from zero to about SGD8, and your card issuer may also charge a fee. Check the displayed fee before proceeding.

### Is tipping expected?

No. Tipping is not expected, and restaurants and hotels often include a service charge.
