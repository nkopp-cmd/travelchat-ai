Mong Kok Computer Centre

Plan a free day around Mong Kok Computer Centre

Mong Kok Computer Centre is a shopping in Mong Kok, Hong Kong, rated Hidden Gem (Localley Score 5/6) and ranked 2nd of 264 rated places in Hong Kong. The closest rated spot is Sai Yeung Choi Street, 90 m away. At least 6 rated spots are within a 10-minute walk.

Mong Kok Computer Centre sits at the corner of Nelson Street and Fa Yuen Street. It has housed computer shops since 1991, in a building that was once the Victoria cinema. Go here when you need a part, a cable or a quick fix, not for a polished mall experience.

The shops sell computer parts and accessories. It works well for phone cases, screen covers, cables and small laptop add-ons. Walk the floors first and compare a few stalls before you buy anything. Most shops are small, so you can cover the building in an hour.

The mall is a short walk from Mong Kok MTR station, exits D3 and E2. Opening hours differ by shop, so aim for the afternoon or early evening when most counters are staffed. Bring your device if you want a repair quote.

What to get

  • Browse computer parts and accessories
  • Pick up cables, phone cases and screen covers
  • Ask about device repairs
  • Compare prices across several shops

Best time

Afternoon to early evening, when shops are staffed

How long

1 hour

Tips

  • Use MTR Mong Kok exit D3 or E2
  • Check two or three shops before you buy
  • Bring your device for a repair quote
  • Pay attention to the product box and warranty
